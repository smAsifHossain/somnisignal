How SomniSignal analyzes an overnight ECG.

01

Validate the recording

The app checks duration, sample rate, missing values, clipping, and whether the file contains a usable ECG channel.

file → quality check
02

Detect each heartbeat

QRS peaks are located and converted into beat-to-beat intervals. Implausible or isolated noisy intervals are filtered.

ECG → beats → RR intervals
03

Measure overnight patterns

Overlapping five-minute windows measure heart-rate variability and frequency patterns associated with apnea events.

RR intervals → HRV features
04

Report the prediction

The model combines the window estimates into a lower- or elevated-risk prediction with clear next-step guidance.

patterns → nightly prediction

understanding your prediction

A clear risk prediction, with appropriate clinical context.

↑

Elevated screening pattern

The ECG contains enough apnea-like heart-rate patterns to suggest elevated risk. This supports seeking a professional sleep evaluation; it does not confirm apnea.

next: discuss PSG or HSAT
✓

Low screening pattern

The model found very few apnea-like minutes. This lowers the research estimate, but it cannot rule out apnea when symptoms remain.

next: consider symptoms
↕

Borderline prediction

When the nightly burden falls between the primary thresholds, the research score determines whether the prediction is reported as lower or elevated risk.

borderline burden → score prediction
!

Prediction unavailable

Only an unreadable recording or failed signal-quality check prevents prediction. A better recording is required before analysis can continue.

quality fail → no prediction
!

This cannot diagnose or rule out sleep apnea. Discuss symptoms and appropriate polysomnography or technically adequate home sleep-apnea testing with a qualified clinician.
